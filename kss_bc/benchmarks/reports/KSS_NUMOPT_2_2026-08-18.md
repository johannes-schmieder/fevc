## Retained architecture and byte inventory

The production path remains one-process and matrix-free. It never forms an
`F x F` dense matrix or observation-space matrix. The persistent compact-cell
inventory is

```text
8 * (9*C + 3*W + 4*F) bytes
```

for seven payload columns, two cell-order vectors, and worker/firm panels and
masses. Deletion units and target strata cost `32*G` and `32*S`; the compact
routed working vectors cost `8*5*(W+F)`. Raw Stata rows, sorting/compression,
transition, hierarchy, solve-ahead, certificates, output, restoration, and
process residency remain separately charged. The model uses the finalized
CZ18 observations `G/C=1` and `S/C=1` (311,730 each) as replaceable target
assumptions.

The compact view supplies transpose, prediction, Schur, worker reconstruction,
worker-to-firm, firm-to-worker, weighted transpose, and diagonal callbacks.
The generic solver still recomputes the complete original worker-plus-firm
normal-equation residual for every RHS. Repeated traversal uses deterministic
cell orders and native segmented aggregation; cancellation-sensitive setup
sums remain compensated.

## Local matched P200 result

The four-processor local fixture has 60,000 stored rows, 10,000 workers,
1,000 firms, 30,000 cells/deletion units, 33,529 strata, seed 8,675,309,
diagonal routing, and 601 complete RHSs. One cold run preceded three warm
runs for each immutable source.

| Source | Cold seconds | Warm seconds | Warm median |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Optimization II | 28.177 | 27.992 / 28.003 / 27.974 | 27.992 |
| Optimization III | 18.246 | 17.825 / 17.848 / 17.889 | 17.848 |

The median gain is 36.24%. Both sources used 92 maximum iterations and 54,074
Schur/preconditioner actions. The largest relative difference among exported
results is `1.11e-16`; every identity and complete-residual gate passed. The
candidate's observed local peak fell from roughly 320 MB to 312 MB on the
warm runs. The retained CSVs and validator receipt are under
`evidence/KSS_NUMOPT_2_2026-08-18/local/`.

A separate commit-bound calibration held the fixture and all 54,074 actions
fixed while varying `batch()` over 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16. Numerical work took
42.796, 30.804, 22.390, 17.774, and 16.418 seconds, respectively. Relative to
batch 16, the measured work factors are 2.607, 1.876, 1.364, 1.083, and 1.000.
All five widths reproduced the reference result and passed the complete
residual and target-identity gates. The target model therefore selects the
largest memory-admitted calibrated batch and charges its measured work
factor; it does not assume that reducing scratch is free.

## Scale model contract

`model_numopt2.py` reads only externally validated aggregate receipts. It
fits strong compressed P20 rungs at target fractions 1/64, 1/32, and 1/16
separately for each density. The central 1/8 rung is an out-of-sample check.
Any field with more than 20% holdout error switches from the simple affine
projection to a last-segment growth transfer and widens the reported range.

Raw `R/C=8` measurements identify incremental import/restoration cost per
extra row and compression cost per incremental `R log2(R)` work. Aggregate
resource receipts calibrate raw Stata bytes per row. The memory projection
then evaluates the Resource API 8 lifecycle formula directly for raw rows,
compact cells, deletion units, strata, output, preservation, runtime, batch
scratch, and the fitted routed hierarchy. The registered `R/C=26.3` case
receives at least 35% model error because it lies beyond the measured raw
ratio. P20 post-setup numerical work scales to P200 by the complete-RHS ratio
`601/61`; reported Schur actions use the same explicit extrapolation. Every
forecast has at least 20% model error, followed by a further 20% admission
headroom. Weak-connectivity results are a sensitivity forecast, not a claim
that one synthetic graph represents all adverse national networks.
