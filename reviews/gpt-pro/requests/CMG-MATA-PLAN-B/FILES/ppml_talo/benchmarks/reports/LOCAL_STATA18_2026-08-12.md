# Local Stata 18 qualification report — 12 August 2026

## Status and scope

**Local qualification result: PASS.** The result applies to the supported
pure two-way worker--firm command on Stata/MP 18 for Apple Silicon. The source
under test was the clean commit
`06137ded3332b860178d556e7a91ca77dad5a4d2`.

This report does not close ST7 or ST8. Stata/MP 19 Linux portability, SCC
scheduler evidence, and named human review remain open. The benchmark is
finite numerical and performance evidence, not an asymptotic theorem or an
empirical-network applicability certificate.

## Repeatability

Two independent one-million-row runs used the same source, seeds, and options.
Their CSV rows were identical in every field except wall time (`54.283` versus
`54.254` seconds). This includes all 16 plug-in, correction, TALO, and trace
MCSE values; solver diagnostics; leverage diagnostics; and metadata. Peak RSS
was 2.058 and 2.117 decimal GB.
