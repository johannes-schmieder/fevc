## Replay and cross-platform comparison

The base 1m task and independent same-host job `7148701` both ran on
`scc-ne4.scc.bu.edu`. Every CSV field except wall time was byte-identical. Wall
times were 266.209 and 273.601 seconds. Both qacct records report four slots
and clean exits.

An additional repeat, job `7148688`, ran on `scc-h32.scc.bu.edu`. Nine
floating fields differed at the last stored digits: the maximum absolute
difference among the 16 plug-in, correction, TALO, and MCSE fields was
`1.50e-20`; the maximum-residual difference was `2.45e-17`. This cross-node
run is numerically stable but does not satisfy the byte-exact same-host replay
criterion, so the two results are classified separately.

Against the retained Stata 18 Apple-Silicon ladder, the largest absolute
difference among the same 16 result fields at any size was `8.40e-15` (at
10m). SCC Stata time was 4.57 to 6.53 times the local Stata time across the
ladder. These differences are recorded rather than silently treated as exact
cross-platform equality.
